Frequently asked questions

Does Source Stays receive commission when I register?

Registration alone does not confirm a commission. Any commission depends on Zylus's current referral rules, successful attribution and eligibility conditions. Confirm these with Zylus.

Is cashback or a return guaranteed?

No guarantee is made on this page. Check the current official programme terms for cashback eligibility, timing, investment risks and any returns before committing funds.

Where should I submit my application and payment?

Use the official Zylus RECB portal and its payment instructions. Source Stays does not collect investment funds or sensitive registration documents through this page.

What if I cannot enter the consultant CID?

Do not assume the referral has been recorded. Contact Zylus and ask them to confirm the application is attributed to Source Stays, CID 612675, before paying.
