Hotel booking questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I find a hotel in a particular area?

Use the location search near the top of this page. Enter an area such as Ikeja, Lekki or Magodo to narrow the published hotel listings.

Can I choose a room category?

Open the hotel listing to review its room categories and displayed customer rates, where available.

Is a displayed room rate confirmation of availability?

No. Room rates and availability can change. Send your preferred dates and room details to Source Stays so availability can be confirmed before payment.

How do I make a booking enquiry?

Use the booking or enquiry options on the hotel property page, or contact Source Stays through WhatsApp with your dates, room type and guest details.
