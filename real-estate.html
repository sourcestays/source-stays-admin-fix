Frequently asked questions

How do I search for a property in a particular area?

Choose the property category and enter an area such as Magodo, Ikeja or Lekki in the search field. The results show published listings matching your selection.

Can I inspect a property before making a decision?

Contact Source Stays through the enquiry option on the listing or WhatsApp to discuss inspection arrangements and the next steps.

How do I enquire about a property for sale or land?

Open the listing and use its enquiry option. Our team can guide you on the available information and the appropriate next steps.

Do you manage properties for owners?

Contact us with your property location and management requirements so we can discuss the services and terms that may be suitable.

Are all listings guaranteed to be available?

Listings and property terms can change. Please contact Source Stays to confirm current status, price, documentation information and any inspection or transaction requirements before proceeding.
